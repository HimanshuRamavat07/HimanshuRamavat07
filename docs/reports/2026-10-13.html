📚 Research Worth Reading

ReplaySSM: Cache SSM Inputs, Not State

Problem: Hybrid state-space model (SSM) decode steps spend bandwidth repeatedly reading and writing large recurrent states, limiting throughput and complicating speculative rollback.

Key idea: ReplaySSM keeps a checkpoint plus a ring buffer of recent SSM inputs. Full state updates flush periodically while outputs reconstruct from cached inputs.

Why developers should care: The authors report up to about 1.47× faster standard decode on hybrid models. Speculative throughput on 120B-class stacks in vLLM rose up to about 2.10× at large batches.

Paper: arXiv:2610.13778
