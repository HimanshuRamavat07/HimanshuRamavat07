MIT posts independent Lean checks on claimed Riemann Hypothesis proof

Impact: 5/5Impact: 5 out of 5  |  Category: Research / Models

What happened: On November 21, 2026, MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland published live verification results for a Lean proof announced November 20. All 175,187 modules compiled, and nanoda and con-leche accepted the full export.

Why it matters: The page states Mathlib's Riemann zeta definition, limits generalized results to Dirichlet L-functions, and still lists official Comparator and paranoid-kernel replays as running. Machine checks do not settle community mathematical acceptance.

Monitor it — Track Comparator completion and human expert review before citing RH as settled in product copy.

Source: MIT verification page · TechCrunch

Signal blocks OS-level AI agents from reading in-app screen content

Impact: 5/5Impact: 5 out of 5  |  Category: Security / Agents

What happened: On November 20, 2026, Signal announced default screenshot blocking on iOS 27 and Android when assistants try to read the app. Siri AI blocking defaults on iPhone; Android enables Screen security after assistant access is detected.

Why it matters: Signal warns disabled protection may expose chats to Gemini, Siri, and other OS agents and break its privacy guarantees. macOS lacks reliable screenshot blocking; Excluded Apps under Siri settings is the interim step.

Adopt it — Audit messaging apps for similar OS read paths before shipping regulated or healthcare chat features.

Source: Signal · Signal Support

OpenAI documents layered defenses against adversarial distillation attacks

Impact: 5/5Impact: 5 out of 5  |  Category: Security / Models

What happened: On November 20, 2026, OpenAI published countermeasures against unauthorized distillation of frontier capabilities. GPT-6.2 Astra cut adaptive protected-reasoning extraction success from 54.0% to 0.2% on earlier-request attacks.

Why it matters: OpenAI ties protected reasoning to conversation history, drops reasoning after message rewrites, and blocks older model families from reading GPT-6.2 traces. Campaign-level enforcement removed thousands to tens of thousands of accounts weekly this year.

Monitor it — Treat visible answers and tool arguments as leakage surfaces when fine-tuning on third-party model outputs.

Source: OpenAI

Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini for local agentic coding

Impact: 4/5Impact: 4 out of 5  |  Category: Models

What happened: On November 20, 2026, Thinking Machines Lab shipped Inkling 2 Mini under Apache 2.0. The looped MoE model has 26.4B total parameters, 3.4B active, a 1M-token context window, and multimodal text and image input.

Why it matters: The team reports 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 and a roughly 20GB mixed-precision checkpoint for local inference. Looping applies middle layers three times per token with less written chain-of-thought.

Try it — Benchmark Inkling 2 Mini on one private repo before swapping a cloud coding agent offline.

Source: Thinking Machines · Hugging Face

OpenAI ships MedChemBench for medicinal chemistry reasoning evaluation

Impact: 4/5Impact: 4 out of 5  |  Category: Research / Models

What happened: On November 20, 2026, OpenAI released MedChemBench with 99 problems drawn from 117 papers and patents. GPT-6.2 Astra earned 84.5% of available credit at the shared xhigh reasoning setting across structure, explanation, selection, and property tasks.

Why it matters: OpenAI states results do not prove models can discover drugs or improve lab decisions. Prior exposure to published sources cannot be ruled out, and each attempt ran without tools or new experiments.

Try it — Run MedChemBench on internal chemistry RAG stacks before pitching AI compound triage to stakeholders.

Source: OpenAI

OS-integrated agents create an app-layer privacy crisis

What's changing: Apple, Google, and Microsoft embed assistants that scan on-screen content by default. Signal blocked capture inside its mobile app on November 20, 2026.

Why it matters: End-to-end encryption loses meaning once OS agents read plaintext after decryption. Messaging, banking, and health apps cannot rely on transport security alone.

Watch next: Platform APIs that let developers opt apps out of AI screen and filesystem access without breaking core features.

AI Security Watch

Issue: Signal blocked default OS assistant screen reads and in-app screenshots on mobile after Gemini and Siri gained on-screen awareness.

Impact: Users who disable Screen security may expose encrypted chats to OS-level agents and break Signal's stated privacy guarantees for all thread participants.

Who should care: Mobile engineers, privacy-focused product teams, and compliance leads for messaging or health apps.

Recommended action: Keep Screen security enabled on Signal 8.35.1+ Android and 8.37+ iOS 27. Document risks before allowing screenshots in regulated workflows.

Impact: Attackers could clone cyber or biology-adjacent skills into unsafeguarded student models by harvesting tool-call arguments and hidden reasoning traces.
