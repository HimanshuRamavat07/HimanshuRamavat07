📚 Research Worth Reading

JevAgentHarness: Benchmarking, Deploying and Training Decision Models That Share Control with LLMs

Problem: Agent harnesses call full LLMs for many multiple-choice tool steps, inflating latency and cost without improving success.

Key idea: The authors fit confidence thresholds on agent trajectories so small decision models handle safe branches while LLMs retain hard cases.

Why developers should care: Their AgentDecision-4B model cut agent spend up to 89% with under three-point success loss on held-out tasks in reported runs.

Paper: arXiv:2610.21412

Stateless Language Agents: Scaling Long-Horizon Automated Research

Problem: Long-horizon research agents replay growing histories, duplicate experiments, and burn tokens without measurable progress.

Key idea: Stateless Language Agents keep durable state in a harness that assigns fresh workers from summarized evidence each step.

Why developers should care: The SLA framework reached strong kernel baselines with over 84% fewer tokens than compared frameworks at billion-token budgets.

Paper: arXiv:2610.07625

From Outcomes to Steps: Reliable Success-Aware Critic Learning for Long-Horizon Language Agents

Problem: Terminal-only rewards make critics miss whether intermediate actions actually raise eventual task success under sparse feedback.

Key idea: SCALE adds Success Value predictions and Success-GAE credit while keeping token-level PPO updates for language agents.

Why developers should care: Reported gains reach 4.1 percentage points in success rate across five agent benchmarks and three model backbones.

Paper: arXiv:2610.27623
