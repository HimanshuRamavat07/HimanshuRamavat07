📚 Research Worth Reading

CPU-ARC: Agentic Reasoning Caching for on-CPU Reasoning Reuse

Problem: Large language models repeat similar reasoning steps on every new instance of a solved task class.

Key idea: The authors compile reasoning traces into small CPU-executable agentic reasoning caches that replay solutions with little model involvement.

Why developers should care: The paper reports 16–291× lower invocation cost than the source model on hard math and science tasks with explainable cache units.

Paper: arXiv:2611.09891

Strategist: Evolving Reasoning with Agents

Problem: Hand-written inference strategies do not transfer across tasks, so each new agent workflow starts from scratch.

Key idea: A meta-agent evolves executable strategy blueprints, tests them on development samples, and stores results in a reusable library.

Why developers should care: On 30 benchmarks, Strategist matches the strongest baseline accuracy at 39% lower inference cost and transfers strategies to new models.

Paper: arXiv:2611.03618
