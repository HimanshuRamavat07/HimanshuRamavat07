Why it matters: LiteLLM is a common LLM routing proxy in agent frameworks, MCP servers, and CI pipelines. If your environment ever installed versions 1.82.7 or 1.82.8 — or ran CI with the compromised Trivy action between March 19–24 — assume all secrets reachable to those runners are compromised. The five-month gap between attack and archive surfacing means dormant exfiltrated credentials may still be active.

Adopt it — audit for LiteLLM 1.82.7/1.82.8, check for litellm_init.pth and sysmon.service persistence, rotate all LLM and cloud credentials
