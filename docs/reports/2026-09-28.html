REDAgentBench: Executable Red Teaming and Faithful Measurement of LLM Agent Systems

Problem: Single attack-success rates hide whether agents actually changed external state.

Key idea: Run 1,661 sandboxed cases and judge harm from service receipts, not chat transcripts alone.

Why developers should care: Macro-average attack success hit 65.69%, and many violations followed explicit safety statements.
