Google splits Gemini chat history from generative AI training controls

Impact: ⭐⭐⭐⭐Impact: 4 out of 5  |  Category: Business / Developer Tools

What happened: On November 16, 2026, Google announced new Gemini app settings that separate saved chat history from use of chat content for generative AI model training. The rollout is global over the coming weeks.

Why it matters: Users can resume prior chats while opting out of future training on prompts, attachments, and tool calls. When history is off, Google will retain future chats for around two months for safety operations, up from about 72 hours.

📚 Research Worth Reading

Coding Agent Memory Post-Training II (CAMG-II)

Problem: Long-horizon agents exceed active context and need policies for what to write to disk and when to read it back.

Key idea: CAMG-II trains one policy across five gyms with asynchronous on-policy distillation from a teacher that already knows file operations.

Why developers should care: The authors report 2.97× higher gain than the strongest OPD baseline and transfers to Terminal-Bench 2.1 and SWE-Bench Pro V2.

Paper: arXiv:2611.14009

Can LLM Agents Organize Their Own Memory? (SoMem)

Problem: Fixed memory schemas struggle when tasks and interaction patterns evolve after deployment.

Key idea: SoMem stores linked documents where the agent chooses summaries, groupings, and revisions while retaining links to source interactions.

Why developers should care: SoMem beats the strongest baseline by 2.66–3.10 points on long-dialog benchmarks and reuses memory across models.

Paper: arXiv:2611.11929
