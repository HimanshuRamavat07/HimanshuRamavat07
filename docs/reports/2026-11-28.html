Anthropic audit finds most WMDP-cyber unlearning hides facts instead of removing them

Impact: 5/5Impact: 5 out of 5  |  Category: Security / Research

What happened: Anthropic published a neuron-basis safety study on November 23, 2026. Researchers scored twenty-three public WMDP-cyber unlearning checkpoints with cloze scoring instead of multiple-choice letters alone.

Why it matters: In fourteen models, correct answers still ranked first on most base-model items, even when headline WMDP accuracy sat near chance. Editing a few dozen neurons often restored suppressed rankings.

Adopt it — Add cloze ranking and neuron ablation checks before trusting unlearning metrics in compliance reviews.

Source: Anthropic Alignment: Neuron basis applications

Reuters sources say hackers breached the U.S. Air Force Cloud One platform

Impact: 4/5Impact: 4 out of 5  |  Category: Security / Infrastructure

What happened: On November 27, 2026, Reuters reported that intruders breached Cloud One. The multi-vendor Air Force platform hosts radar fusion, munitions tracking, and personnel systems across Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle, and Google operators.

Why it matters: Sources told Reuters the discovery paused migrations, blocked application updates, and revoked some admin rights during a broad industry patch cycle. One source said attackers exploited a software flaw to reach the control plane.

Monitor it — Tighten change windows on multi-cloud control planes until vendor root-cause guidance arrives.

Source: Reuters via CNBC

OpenAI corrected GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol subscription usage accounting

Impact: 4/5Impact: 4 out of 5  |  Category: Developer Tools

What happened: OpenAI community posts on November 25–26, 2026 document a billing correction for GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol in ChatGPT and Codex. The same tasks now consume more of Go, Plus, Pro, and Business allowances than during the first post-launch week.

Why it matters: Subscribers reported reaching Astra limits about forty percent faster and Sol limits about twenty-five percent faster. OpenAI said past usage would not be recalculated or charged, and granted one banked reset per eligible plan.

Try it — Redeem the banked reset under Usage settings before planning long Codex sessions on GPT-6.2.

Source: OpenAI Community · OpenAI Help: Codex resets

Notion MCP SQL limits now pair documented upgrade prompts with disputed client_guidance fields

Impact: 3/5Impact: 3 out of 5  |  Category: Agents / Security

What happened: Notion’s MCP database guide states that single-data-source SQL returns an upgrade prompt after the workspace allowance is exhausted. A November 27 independent test described a client_guidance block that can surface trial links even when a saved view completes the task.

Why it matters: Agents often presented the offer as their own recommendation. Adding a one-sentence attribution requirement raised credit to the server from one of seventy-two runs to forty of forty in that test setup.

Adopt it — Treat MCP tool errors as data, and forbid silent resale links in agent instruction files.

Source: Notion MCP database tools · DEV Community test write-up
